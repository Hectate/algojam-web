What is it?

AlgoJam is a relaxed, low-pressure jam about procedural generation. Build a level generator, a story engine, a music machine, a plant grower, a poem bot: anything where you make the thing that makes the thing. Computers are great tools to enable human creativity, and that's what this jam is all about!

It follows in the spirit of its older sibling and inspiration, ProcJam, which you should also participate in!

AI Usage

  • Generative AI tools are allowed as assistants, for example to help write or debug code, explain an algorithm, or bounce ideas around.
  • Your submission should show your own creativity. The idea, the design and the generator itself should come from you.
  • Entries that are mostly AI output, such as software an agent designed and built from a prompt, or AI-generated images, music or text submitted as-is, aren't in the spirit of the jam and may be removed.
  • Say how you used AI tools. A sentence or two on your submission page is enough.
